# OfficeHub Approval Policy

## Discount Approvals

Any discount above 10% for regular customers or 15% for preferred customers requires manager approval.

## High-Value Orders

Orders exceeding $5,000 require sales manager approval.

## Refund Approvals

Refunds above $500 require finance manager approval.

## Exception Handling

All exceptions to standard policies must be documented and approved by a department head.
