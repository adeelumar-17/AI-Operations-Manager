# OfficeHub Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days after order confirmation.

## Express Shipping

Express shipping is available for an additional fee and delivers within 2-3 business days.

## Free Shipping Threshold

Orders over $100 qualify for free standard shipping.

## Damaged Shipments

If a shipment arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 48 hours for a replacement or refund.
