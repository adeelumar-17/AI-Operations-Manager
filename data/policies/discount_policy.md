# OfficeHub Discount Policy

## Regular Customers

Regular customers may receive a discount of up to 10% on a standard order without manager approval.

## Preferred Customers

Preferred customers may receive a discount of up to 15% without manager approval.

## Discounts Above the Limit

Any discount above the customer's normal limit requires manager approval before the quotation is sent to the customer.

## Maximum Discount

No customer may receive a discount greater than 25%.
