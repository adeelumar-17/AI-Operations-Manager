# OfficeHub Return Policy

## Return Eligibility

Products may be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original condition.

## Return Process

The customer must contact support to initiate a return and receive a return shipping label.

## Refund Timeline

Refunds are processed within 10 business days of receiving the returned item.

## Damaged or Defective Items

Damaged or defective items may be returned at any time for replacement or refund.
