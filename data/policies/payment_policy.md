# OfficeHub Payment Policy

## Payment Methods

We accept credit cards, bank transfers, and purchase orders from corporate customers.

## Payment Terms

Net 30 days for corporate customers. Credit card payments are processed immediately upon order confirmation.

## Invoice Timeline

An invoice is generated immediately after order confirmation and is due within 30 days.

## Late Payments

Payments more than 30 days overdue will incur a 1.5% monthly interest charge.
